[synthadoc](https://github.com/axoviq-ai/synthadoc)

Synthadoc reads your raw source documents — PDFs, spreadsheets, PPTs, web pages, images, videos, Word files, TXTs, and AI session transcripts (.jsonl) — and uses an LLM to synthesize them into a persistent, structured wiki. Cross-references are built automatically, contradictions are detected and surfaced, orphan pages are flagged, and every answer cites its sources
